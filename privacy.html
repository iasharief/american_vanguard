Privacy

Plain privacy policy.

This static classroom site does not send form entries to a server. Entries are saved in the visitor's browser so the demo can show a confirmation without pretending to run campaign software.

What this site collects

The demo forms ask for name, email, ZIP code, and a few campaign preferences such as volunteer role, RSVP, or pledge amount.

Where it goes

In this GitHub Pages version, form entries stay in the visitor's browser storage. A real campaign should connect forms to a trusted service, publish a full privacy policy, and protect supporter data.

Donations

The donation page records classroom pledge interest only. It does not process payments, store card numbers, or create real campaign receipts.
