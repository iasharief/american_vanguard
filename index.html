The point

America needs the long game again.

American Vanguard believes success should be measured by whether the country is stronger, safer, freer, and more independent in the long run. Not by whether politicians can claim a quick win before the next election.

The United States should lead the world because the alternative is worse. But leadership does not mean endless ground wars or pretending every crisis needs American troops on the front line. If force is used, it needs a transparent goal, a real reason, and a clear exit.
