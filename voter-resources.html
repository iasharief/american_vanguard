Voter Resources

Know how to vote before the hallway gets loud.

Confirm the final election rules with your teacher or class organizer. This page keeps the campaign's guidance clear without making up dates.

Before voting

  • Check the official class election date and voting window.
  • Read the party platforms, not just the loudest quotes.
  • Ask each campaign what they would actually prioritize first.

When comparing parties

  • LIVE centers climate policy and green capitalism.
  • Progressive Independents center social liberalism and sharp cuts to government spending.
  • American Vanguard centers global leadership and domestic strength.

Election day

  • Bring whatever your class requires for voting.
  • Help one undecided voter find the platform page.
  • Vote for the party you trust to think beyond quick wins.

Still deciding?

Start with the preamble. It explains the heart of the campaign better than any one-liner can.
